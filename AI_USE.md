# AI use disclosure

I used OpenAI Codex for this assignment, building on the instructor's
[starter repository](https://github.com/anmolsingh0219/FRE-GY-7871A-Assignment1).
I supplied the course materials and detailed analytical instructions, required
separate treatment of negative sentiment and uncertainty, specified that the
implementation follow the assigned Equation 1 and methodological safeguards,
and directed additional source checks and the final submission review. I also
requested use of the professor-recommended EdgarTools resources.
After receiving the additional instructions, I required verification of the
March 2026 dictionary, holdings rankings excluding Item 1A, and an investment
suggestion connected to each analysis.
I also requested a company appendix before the rankings, including public
status and the companies' passage through the data filters.

Codex generated substantial portions of the implementation, notebook, and report
drafts. Its assistance included reviewing sources, acquiring public data,
resolving issuer mappings, implementing and debugging the scoring and statistical
analysis, producing exhibits, and writing and running automated checks. Those
checks were executed through Codex's tools. Corrections included removed-word
dictionary flags, filing-date alignment, issuer and share-class mismatches, and
CSV/XML parsing. An EdgarTools MCP check independently matched AMD and one
filing's metadata. This disclosure describes the assistance used; the empirical
results come from the downloaded observations, with synthetic examples confined
to validation tests.
Codex also implemented the section extraction and ranking calculations and
helped draft their investment implications. These suggestions interpret the
historical evidence; they are not results of a tested trading strategy.
